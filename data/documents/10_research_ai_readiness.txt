RESEARCH PAPER SUMMARY

Title: From digitalization maturity to AI readiness: implications for organizational performance in SMEs in a Swedish county
Author: Einav Peretz Andersson
Journal: Information Systems and e-Business Management
Year: 2026

Summary:
This open-access study examines the relationship between digitalization maturity, AI readiness, and organizational performance in small and medium-sized enterprises.

The study is relevant to AI strategy because organizations may need a foundation of digital capabilities before advanced AI can produce business value. Digital infrastructure, organizational practices, data capabilities, and readiness can affect the ability of SMEs to adopt AI effectively.

Business implication:
A company should assess its current digital maturity before launching complex AI projects. Improving data quality, processes, digital skills, and infrastructure may be necessary prerequisites for successful AI adoption.

RAG test questions:
1. What is the relationship examined by the study?
2. Why can digital maturity matter before AI adoption?
3. What capabilities can support AI readiness?
