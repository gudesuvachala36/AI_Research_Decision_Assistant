BUSINESS ARTICLE: AI FOR BUSINESS DECISION-MAKING

Title: AI as a Decision-Support System

Business decisions often require information from many sources. Managers may need to compare sales trends, customer behavior, operational costs, market conditions, and strategic goals.

An AI decision-support system can retrieve relevant business documents and data, summarize evidence, compare alternatives, and present risks. A RAG-based assistant can answer questions from internal reports while linking recommendations to supporting passages.

For example, a manager might ask: "Should we expand this product into a new region?" A useful assistant could retrieve sales reports, customer research, cost information, and previous expansion analyses. It could then organize evidence into opportunities, risks, assumptions, and unanswered questions.

AI should not automatically replace managerial judgment. Business recommendations can depend on incomplete data, changing market conditions, and organizational constraints.

A strong decision-support workflow therefore includes evidence retrieval, analysis, uncertainty identification, and human review.

Key lesson:
The value of AI decision support comes from helping people make better-informed decisions, not from eliminating human responsibility.
