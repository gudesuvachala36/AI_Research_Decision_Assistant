BUSINESS ARTICLE: AI BUSINESS STRATEGY

Title: From AI Experimentation to Business Value

Artificial intelligence is becoming a business capability rather than a standalone technology project. Organizations can use AI for customer service, forecasting, marketing, software development, supply-chain planning, fraud detection, and decision support.

A successful AI strategy begins with a business problem. Companies should identify a measurable outcome such as reducing service response time, improving forecast accuracy, increasing conversion, or reducing operational cost. Selecting a model before defining the business problem can result in expensive experiments with limited value.

Data is another foundation. AI systems depend on accessible, reliable, well-governed data. Organizations should define ownership, quality standards, security requirements, and processes for updating important datasets.

Business leaders should also decide where humans remain responsible. AI can recommend actions, summarize information, identify patterns, or automate repetitive tasks, while employees can handle exceptions, judgment, and accountability.

A practical AI strategy therefore connects five elements: business objectives, data, technology, people, and governance.

Key lesson:
The strongest AI programs focus on measurable business outcomes instead of treating AI adoption itself as the goal.
